# Design System

## Brand

Raj Hamal

## Design goal

Premium professional analytics portfolio.

## Visual principles

1. Clarity over decoration
2. Evidence over hype
3. Whitespace over density
4. Data visualization as communication, not decoration
5. Subtle motion
6. Strong typography
7. Consistent spacing

## Color direction

Background:
White / off-white

Primary text:
Near-black / charcoal

Secondary text:
Muted gray

Primary accent:
Professional blue

Secondary accent:
Restrained green for data/analytics states

Avoid neon palettes.

## Typography

Primary:
Inter

Optional display:
Space Grotesk

## Components

- Navbar

- Hero

- Certification card
- Education card
- CTA
- Contact form
- Footer
- Tableau embed container

## Cards

Use subtle borders, restrained radius, and minimal shadows.

Do not make every section a floating glass card.

## Motion

Use Framer Motion for:

- reveal animations
- hover states
- route transitions
- subtle timeline animations

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Layout

Use a strong max-width container.

Desktop:
generous whitespace and multi-column layouts.

Mobile:
single-column layouts with comfortable touch targets.

## Accessibility

- visible focus
- semantic headings
- accessible buttons
- sufficient contrast
- keyboard navigation
- descriptive links
- alt text

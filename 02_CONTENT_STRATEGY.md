**Data Analyst | Applied AI & Data Analytics**

## Supporting message

## Career narrative

Raj is not presented as someone starting from zero.

His previous experience provides practical exposure to:

- operations
- customer behaviour
- logistics
- suppliers
- financial records
- reporting
- team leadership
- international clients
- time-sensitive decision-making

His postgraduate study and independent projects add:

- data analytics
- SQL
- Tableau
- Excel/Google Sheets
- R
- business analysis
- applied AI
- emerging technology

## Tone

- professional
- factual
- concise
- evidence-based
- confident without exaggeration

Avoid:

- motivational clichés
- inflated seniority
- vague AI buzzwords
- unsupported impact claims
- fake quantitative achievements

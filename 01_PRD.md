# Product Requirements Document

## Product

Raj Hamal Professional Portfolio

## Domain

`rajhamal.com.np`

## Product statement

A premium professional portfolio that presents Raj Hamal's transition into Data Analytics and Applied AI through verified education, experience, analytics projects, visualizations, and professional evidence.

## Users

### Primary
- Recruiters
- Hiring managers
- Data/BI/AI employers
- Graduate hiring teams

### Secondary
- Professional peers
- Potential clients
- Academic/professional contacts

## User goals

Visitors should quickly answer:

1. Who is Raj?
2. What role is he pursuing?
3. What is his current education?
4. What professional experience does he have?
5. What analytics projects has he built?
6. What tools does he use?
7. How can they contact him?

## Business goals

- Establish a credible professional identity
- Support applications for data/analytics/AI roles
- Showcase project evidence
- Connect tourism/business experience with analytics
- Create a foundation for future CMS-driven content

## Functional requirements

### Navigation
- Responsive navigation
- Home
- About
- Experience
- Projects
- Skills
- Resume
- Contact

### Projects
- Project listing
- Featured project cards
- Dynamic project pages
- GitHub links
- Tableau links/embeds where applicable
- Tools and methodology
- Findings and recommendations only when verified

### Resume
- Dedicated resume page
- Download CTA
- Future support for multiple versions

### Contact
- Professional contact details
- Contact form UI
- Validation
- Backend integration can be added later

### Responsive
Support mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop and large desktop.

## Non-functional requirements

- WCAG 2.2 AA target
- Lighthouse target 95+ where practical
- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard accessibility
- Reduced-motion support
- Optimized images
- SEO-ready routes and metadata
- No horizontal overflow

## Success criteria

A recruiter can understand Raj's profile and reach his strongest project evidence without navigating through unnecessary pages.

The site must remain credible under technical interview scrutiny.
